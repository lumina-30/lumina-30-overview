Open Research Questions

Research agenda derived from the public stack · not claims already resolved by the papers

Interpretation rule: the questions below identify areas for further research. Their inclusion does not mean the current papers have already solved or empirically validated them.

1. Measuring effective independence

  • How should authority domains be counted when they share cloud infrastructure, model providers, credentials, root administrators, or legal control?
  • What evidence is sufficient to distinguish organizational separation from causal independence?

2. Dynamic refusal margins

  • How should refusal latency be updated when model speed, automation depth, or deployment topology changes?
  • How much safety margin is required when detection and verification latency are uncertain rather than fixed?

3. Objective-persistence evidence

  • What qualifies as an external anchor when the evaluator shares data, models, infrastructure, or assumptions with the evaluated system?
  • Can persistence claims remain meaningful after repeated self-modification without collapsing evaluation into internal self-attestation?

4. Human-revisable development

  • Which observable indicators can represent fallback capacity, retained human competence, institutional alternatives, and substitution reversibility?
  • How should revisability be evaluated when no single deployment is irreversible but cumulative dependency is?

5. Cross-layer failure

  • How should a safety case represent a system that passes PCR-C-style infrastructure checks but fails L30-RET because the human decision process is too slow?
  • How should a system be evaluated when refusal remains effective locally while PISF-style authority concentration or societal dependency erodes wider revisability?

6. Empirical program

  • Which real-world governance or infrastructure systems are suitable for non-certifying case studies?
  • How reproducible are RET gate assessments across independent reviewers?
  • Which false-positive and false-negative patterns appear when applying these concepts to operational systems?

Research discipline

The public stack should distinguish conceptual definitions, toy/reference cases, observational studies, and empirical validation. It should also preserve the separation between research evidence and the LUMINA-30 canonical boundary framework.
