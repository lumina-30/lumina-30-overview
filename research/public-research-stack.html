Public Research Stack

Four complementary research lines · control path · objective persistence · effective refusal · pre-irreversibility safety

What this is: a research navigation layer for four public research lines supporting LUMINA-30. The papers do not define the LUMINA-30 canon, do not create certification status, and do not constitute a safety guarantee.

One problem, four different failure locations

The public stack does not treat “AI safety” as a single variable. It separates four questions that can fail independently:

PCR-C

Can the control path still be reached before irreversibility?

Infrastructure-layer cutoff and staged control before intervention capacity is exceeded.

Objective Persistence

How the four lines fit together

PCR-C asks whether the control path remains available. Objective Persistence asks whether persistence claims can be grounded without relying solely on the system being evaluated. L30-RET asks whether Human NO is actually capable of changing the outcome. PISF asks whether irreversible authority, timely refusal, and human-revisable development remain jointly preservable.

They are complementary, not interchangeable. A strong result in one layer does not compensate for a failure in another. The stack is therefore intended for failure-mode separation, not for producing a single certification score.

From paper to research object

The stack is organized around research objects rather than publication count. A reader can enter through the question that matches the problem:

  • Control path: PCR-C research guide
  • Existence condition: Objective Persistence research guide
  • Human refusal: L30-RET research-to-practice guide
  • Integrated safety case: PISF research guide
  • Reproducible examples: Worked Research Cases
  • Unresolved agenda: Open Research Questions

Research-to-practice routing

The research stack can inform LUMINA-30 practical review, but it does not turn the research papers into a certification workflow. Control-path concerns can route toward PCR-C and pre-incident boundary review; refusal concerns toward L30-RET, Stop Authority, and evidence requirements; disputed or failed control toward Incident Review; and broader authority/revisability concerns toward PISF-style safety-case analysis.
