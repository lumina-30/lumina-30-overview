## Interpretation rule ｜ 解釈ルール


**Research supports LUMINA-30; research does not define the canon.**


The public research stack separates four complementary core research lines. Paper 7 / Human Anchor is an additional public formal and implementation-bridge layer, not a fifth interchangeable core line. This stack is not a certification scheme, a compliance standard, a safety guarantee, or a replacement for the LUMINA-30 canonical boundary framework.


公開研究スタックは、LUMINA-30を支える4つの補完的な中核研究ラインを分離して示す。第7論文 / Human Anchorは、5本目の同列な中核ラインではなく、追加の公開形式理論・実装接続レイヤーである。認証制度、適合標準、安全保証、LUMINA-30正典の代替ではない。

## PCR-C


PCR-C is a staged infrastructure control framework focused on preventing systems from crossing irreversibility thresholds after effective intervention capacity has already been exceeded.


LUMINA-30 defines the boundary question; PCR-C provides one infrastructure-layer cutoff model for acting before the boundary is crossed.


PCR-Cは、介入能力を超える前に不可逆閾値を越えることを防ぐための段階的インフラ制御研究である。LUMINA-30の境界定義そのものではない。

## Objective Persistence


The current public research states a narrower conditional result: if an amendment authority can jointly alter the objective, the semantics of objective identity, and the validation basis while preserving a positive verdict, that verdict is not amendment-independent evidence of persistence. Defensible assurance therefore requires either a verified invariant excluding such masking amendments or a persistence-relevant validation basis outside the amendment authority being assessed; that basis need not be physically external.


It does not define an operational checklist, certification state, compliance rule, or institutional mandate.


現行の公開研究は、より限定された条件付き結果を扱う。目的、目的同一性の意味論、検証基盤を同じamendment authorityが共同で変更でき、目的持続性に反する変更を正の判定で覆い隠せる場合、その正の判定はamendment-independentな持続性証拠にはならない。防御可能なassuranceには、そのようなmasking amendmentを排除するverified invariant、または評価対象のamendment authorityから外れたpersistence-relevant validation basisの少なくとも一方が必要であり、その基盤は物理的に外部である必要はない。

## L30-RET


L30-RET — the LUMINA-30 Refusal Effectiveness Test (RET) — asks whether an independent Human NO can still change the outcome before irreversibility.


RET gates:


1. Authority
2. Decision-Relevant Information
3. Practical Access
4. Temporal Sufficiency
5. Causal Efficacy
6. Execution Independence
7. Epistemic Autonomy


A positive RET result is time- and scope-dependent. Human presence, formal approval, or a nominal stop control is not sufficient by itself.


L30-RETは、Human NOが不可逆化前に実際に結果を変えられるかを、7つの非補償的ゲートで検査する。


Version DOI v1.3: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23282593  
Concept DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22980038


## PISF


PISF — Pre-Irreversibility Safety Framework — integrates three non-substitutable safety dimensions:


- A — Distributed Irreversible Authority
- B — Effective Pre-Irreversibility Refusal
- C — Human-Revisable Development Viability


Assurance conditions:


- Boundary Completeness
- Model Adequacy
- Independent Assurance


A/B/C are treated as a maintained property rather than a one-time pass.


PISFは、Authority・Refusal・Revisabilityを同一のpre-irreversibility safety caseで扱う。各次元は相互に代替できない。


Version DOI v1.2: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23283256  
Concept DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23042905


## Paper 7 / Human Anchor / AIS-HA


Paper 7 adds a distinct formal Human Anchor layer focused on protected authority transitions and the preservation of a Human-Revisable Authority Region. Its public implementation surface is experimental and bounded.


Key elements include AIS-HA (AI’s Human Anchor Criterion), Protected Admission, Resource-side Closure, Anti-Bypass, Boundary Completeness, and model-to-reality transfer. The formal claim is model-relative and conditional; the existence of public implementation pages is not evidence of empirical validation, third-party validation, certification, or institutional adoption.


第7論文は、protected authority transitionとHuman-Revisable Authority Regionの維持を扱う独立したHuman Anchor形式理論レイヤーを追加する。公開Human Anchor層は実験的・boundedな実装／検証surfaceであり、理論的妥当性、実装可能性、実装実績、実証、第三者検証を混同しない。

## Worked research cases ｜ 研究例


PISF v1.2 includes four explicit toy/reference cases:


1. R1 — Threshold Release Gate
   - 3-of-5 gate
   - shared-root dependence
   - nominal vs robust irreversible-authority threshold


2. R2 — Time-Bounded Emergency Refusal
   - detection / decision / authorization / enforcement / verification latency
   - refusal margin before the last safe intervention point


3. R3 — Fallback-Capacity Dynamics
   - h(t+1) = h(t) - αa(t) + βm(t)
   - finite-horizon revisability
   - automation/dependency, fallback investment, and horizon sensitivity


4. R4 — Viable but path-locked
   - compares states that both remain viable while preserving different numbers of materially distinct future options
   - shows that viability alone does not establish preservation of materially distinct future choices


These are not real-system empirical validation.


## Cross-paper synthesis ｜ 横断的な読み方


PCR-C asks whether the control path remains reachable.  
Objective Persistence asks whether persistence claims can be grounded without relying solely on the system being evaluated.  
L30-RET asks whether Human NO is causally effective and timely.  
PISF asks whether authority, refusal, and human-revisable development remain jointly preservable.  
Paper 7 asks whether protected authority transitions can remain inside a Human-Revisable Authority Region through an independently enforceable Human Anchor.


PCR-Cは制御経路、Objective Persistenceは目的持続性の根拠、L30-RETはHuman NOの実効性、PISFはAuthority / Refusal / Revisabilityの同時維持を扱う。第7論文は、protected authority transitionを独立したHuman AnchorによりHuman-Revisable Authority Region内へ保てるかを扱う。


A strong result in one layer does not compensate for failure in another.


## Open research agenda ｜ 未解決研究課題


The following are research-agenda questions derived from the public stack, not claims already resolved by the papers:


- measurement of effective authority-domain independence;
- dynamic refusal margins under changing system speed and topology;
- evidence standards for external anchors;
- observable indicators of human-revisable development and fallback capacity;
- cross-layer failure analysis;
- independent-review reproducibility and empirical case-study design.


以下は今後の研究課題であり、現行論文がすでに解決・実証済みという意味ではない。


- authority domainの実効的独立性の測定
- システム速度や構成変化に応じたdynamic refusal margin
- external anchorの証拠基準
- human-revisable development / fallback capacityの観測指標
- レイヤー横断失敗
- 独立reviewer間の再現性とempirical case study設計
