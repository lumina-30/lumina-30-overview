LUMINA-30 Irreversibility Boundary Atlas

Find the point of no practical return — and whether a human NO still works — across ten domains.

Before irreversible AI escalation, could humans still effectively refuse, stop, verify, or redirect?

This atlas is not certification, a safety guarantee, legal advice, or an official determination. CATALOGUED does not mean deeply reviewed or empirically validated, and SCENARIO is not incident evidence.

One-minute application check

1Where is the point after which the consequential action cannot realistically be undone?
2Immediately before that point, who can actually say NO?
3Does that person still have the authority, evidence, and time required for the NO to change the outcome?

Irreversibility boundary

3. Critical infrastructure, energy & transport

ATLAS-03 ｜ Uber developmental automated-driving fatal crash (2018)

CATALOGUEDINCIDENTOBSERVEDAPPLICABLE
Irreversibility boundary
The point during public-road testing when the ADS is controlling the vehicle and the remaining collision-avoidance window falls below effective human intervention time.
Human Final Refusal
Could the safety driver, test operator, management, or testing authority have stopped unsafe testing conditions or autonomous operation before the crash?
Why it is included
The NTSB found operator monitoring failure and also cited inadequate safety risk assessment, ineffective operator oversight, automation-complacency controls, and safety culture at Uber ATG as contributing factors.
Limit / caution
The review target is not only model output, but the operational structure around human intervention.

8. Information space, elections & social decision-making

ATLAS-08 ｜ AI-generated Biden voice robocall before the New Hampshire primary (2024)

CATALOGUEDINCIDENTOBSERVEDAPPLICABLE
Irreversibility boundary
The point where synthetic political audio is distributed close enough to an election that correction and attribution time becomes scarce.
Human Final Refusal
Can carriers, election authorities, law enforcement, and platforms authenticate, contain, and correct the message before voting decisions are affected?
Why it is included
The FCC documented robocalls using an artificially created voice resembling President Biden two days before the 2024 New Hampshire presidential primary and later expanded action on AI-generated political robocalls.
Limit / caution
The boundary question is timing, authenticity, containment, and correction—not a general rule for political speech.

10. AI development infrastructure, model release & authority transfer

ATLAS-10 ｜ Widely available model weights and rollback difficulty

CATALOGUEDSTRUCTURAL_RISKSTRUCTURAL_INFERENCEPARTIAL
Irreversibility boundary
The release point at which advanced model weights become broadly downloadable and the releasing organization can no longer recover copies, redistribution, or downstream modification.
Human Final Refusal
Before release, can developers and institutions genuinely choose additional testing, staged access, conditions, delay, or non-release?
Why it is included
NTIA's 2024 report examined the benefits and risks of dual-use foundation models with widely available weights and recommended continued monitoring as future model capabilities evolve.
Limit / caution
This does not claim that open weights are inherently unsafe; the boundary is the loss of practical recall after release.
