LUMINA-30 · IRREVERSIBILITY BOUNDARY ATLAS

LUMINA-30 Irreversibility Boundary Atlas

Find the point of no practical return — and whether a human NO still works — across ten domains.

Before irreversible AI escalation, could humans still effectively refuse, stop, verify, or redirect?

You do not have to agree with LUMINA-30. Try it on one real decision. Identify the point or intervention window after which the outcome becomes hard to undo, then ask whether a human NO immediately before it still has the authority, evidence, time, and causal force to change what happens next.

This atlas is not certification, a safety guarantee, legal advice, or an official determination. CATALOGUED does not mean deeply reviewed or empirically validated, and SCENARIO is not incident evidence.

One-minute application check

1Where is the point or intervention window after which the consequential outcome cannot realistically be undone?
2Immediately before that point or window, who can actually say NO?
3Does that person still have the authority, evidence, and time required for the NO to change the outcome?

Causal check: If that person says NO, what actually stops, is held, is isolated, is revoked, or otherwise becomes unavailable?

A human-in-the-loop is not enough if the human cannot still change the outcome. If the NO arrives too late, lacks authority, lacks evidence, or does not actually stop execution, human involvement may exist in form while remaining weak in effect.

CATALOGUEDINCIDENTOBSERVEDAPPLICABLE

Human Final Refusal
Could the safety driver, test operator, management, or testing authority have stopped unsafe testing conditions or autonomous operation before the crash?

8. Information space, elections & social decision-making

ATLAS-08 ｜ AI-generated Biden voice robocall before the New Hampshire primary (2024)

CATALOGUEDINCIDENTOBSERVEDAPPLICABLE
Irreversibility boundary
The point where synthetic political audio is distributed close enough to an election that correction and attribution time becomes scarce.
Human Final Refusal
Can carriers, election authorities, law enforcement, and platforms authenticate, contain, and correct the message before voting decisions are affected?
Why it is included
The FCC documented robocalls using an artificially created voice resembling President Biden two days before the 2024 New Hampshire presidential primary and later expanded action on AI-generated political robocalls.
Limit / caution
The boundary question is timing, authenticity, containment, and correction—not a general rule for political speech.

For policymakers, UN and government — try it on one real decision

Use 15 minutes for a first-pass boundary review of one policy, regulation, public-procurement, or deployment decision.

  1. Pick one real decision.
  2. Identify the point after which the consequence becomes difficult to reverse.
  3. Identify the human authority immediately before that point.
  4. Check whether that person still has the authority, evidence, and time needed to change the outcome.
  5. Record the result as YES / NO / UNKNOWN and the next action.
